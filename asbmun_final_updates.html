What is ASBMUN?

The Asian School Bahrain Model United Nations (ASBMUN) is a premier conference designed to foster diplomacy, critical thinking, and global awareness among aspiring young leaders. Delegates gather to engage in rigorous debate, tackle pressing global issues, and craft resolutions in an environment that simulates the authentic dynamics of international relations.

COUNCILS

Committees & Agendas

UNICEF

The United Nations International Children's Emergency Fund is responsible for providing humanitarian and developmental aid to children worldwide. Delegates will focus on protecting child rights in conflict zones and ensuring global educational access.

UNESCO

The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization seeks to build peace through international cooperation in education, sciences, and culture. Discussions will center around the preservation of historical heritage amidst modern warfare.

EU

The European Union is a political and economic union of European states. This committee will simulate the European Parliament, addressing continental economic policies, border security, and regional energy crises.

FCC

The Future Crisis Council is a specialized dynamic committee designed to test delegates' adaptability. Delegates will be plunged into an ongoing, rapidly evolving fictional or historical crisis requiring immediate, unconventional action.

FIA

The Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile governs auto racing events. In a unique MUN twist, this committee will debate the ethics, environmental impact, and future regulations of international motorsports and automotive technologies.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join the ASBMUN?
ASBMUN is open to middle and high school students from across Bahrain and neighboring region institutions. Whether you are a first-time beginner or an experienced delegate, there are committees tailored for all skill levels.
Do I need prior Model UN experience to participate?
Not at all! We welcome delegates of all experience levels. Comprehensive study guides and pre-conference training workshops are provided to help first-time delegates get comfortable with parliamentary procedure and diplomacy.
How do I register for a Chair or Delegate position?
You can register directly through our Registrations page on this website. Simply navigate to the "Registrations" tab, select your desired role (Chair, Delegate, Press, or Runner), and fill out the attached Google Form before the stated deadline.
What is the dress code for the conference?
Formal Western Business Attire or formal national attire is strictly required for all delegates, chairs, press members, and runners throughout all official conference sessions and ceremonies.
